About

Daniel Garriga is a New York photographer working across hospitality and commercial real estate. Born in Barcelona and raised between Berlin and Paris, Daniel trained at NYU’s Tisch School of the Arts and built his career shooting campaigns for brands like Chanel, Oscar de la Renta, Martini, Turkish Airlines, and Apple. He brings that same campaign-level storytelling to architecture and interiors, making spaces feel like somewhere you want to be. His clients include Cushman & Wakefield and boutique hotels such as The Frederick, The Evelyn, The Iroquois and The Belleclaire.

Based in New York. Available to travel.
